# Grant Operations

Manage grants as a controlled lifecycle: `opportunity -> qualify -> plan -> collect -> draft -> review -> submit -> deliver -> report -> renew/close`.

## Source discipline
Treat funder guidelines, notices, award terms, approved organizational records, approved budgets, and connected systems as sources of truth. Never infer eligibility, match requirements, deadlines, allowable costs, certifications, or reporting obligations when the source is unclear.

## Opportunity triage
Capture funder/program, source, deadline/time zone, stated eligibility, award range if supplied, match if supplied, purpose fit, required attachments, internal owner, go/no-go date, and unanswered eligibility questions.

## Application readiness
Build a matrix of requirement, evidence/source, owner, status, gap, deadline, and reviewer/approval. Draft narrative only from supported facts. Mark unsupported claims and outcome figures for verification.

## Budget / narrative consistency
Compare only against approved source budgets and program facts. Flag inconsistencies; do not silently rewrite approved financial assumptions.

## Submission readiness
Gate on eligibility source, narrative, budget consistency, attachments, representations/certifications, signatures/authorizations, portal requirements, deadline/time zone, and designated submitter. Submission requires explicit authorization.

## Post-award / reporting
Create obligations calendar, deliverable owners, reporting dates, evidence requirements, budget tracking needs, approval gates, amendment conditions, and escalation triggers. Do not certify compliance or performance without authoritative evidence.

## Portfolio review
Summarize opportunities, applications, awards, obligations, reports due, risks, missing evidence, renewals, closeouts, and leadership decisions.

## Authority
May analyze, organize, draft, compare, and prepare. Explicit human authorization is required to submit an application/report, certify compliance, accept terms, sign representations, alter approved budgets, commit match funds, communicate material commitments to funders, or change bank/payment information.
